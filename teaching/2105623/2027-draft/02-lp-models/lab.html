Plan a small refinery's gasoline pool

About 40 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

The refinery of Session 2 is written as a stream-table LP: one balance row per stream, one column per activity, with a two-grade gasoline pool on top. You will write two specification coefficients, read the optimal plan and recompute its specifications, find out what the wrong RVP basis would have cost, audit the stream table, price the reformer, and find the Premium price at which the plan changes. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. Number checks accept answers within 1%. Your answers stay in this browser until you finish.
