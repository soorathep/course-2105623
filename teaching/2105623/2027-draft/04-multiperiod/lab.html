What storage costs, and a battery for an office building

About 40 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

Two multiperiod models. First the oil plan of the slides (Food Manufacture, Williams 12.1): you change the cost of storage and explain how much the profit moves. Then an office building with rooftop PV and a battery under the PEA time-of-use tariff: you check the state-of-charge row by hand, read the weekday plan, remove the end-of-day rule, repair a Saturday plan that cannot happen, and change the battery size. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. Number checks accept answers within 1%. Your answers stay in this browser until you finish.

Studying on your own because class was cancelled? Watch the narrated slides for Session 4 first, then come back here.
