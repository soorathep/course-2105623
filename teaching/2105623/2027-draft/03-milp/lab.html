Commit four boilers for one day

About 40 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

A utility plant must decide, hour by hour, which of four boilers are on and how much steam each one makes. A boiler that is on costs a fixed amount per hour and must run between its minimum and maximum load; starting it costs extra. You will write the load and start-up rows, compare the LP relaxation with the MILP, see what a loose and an invalid big-M do, check the solver by enumerating one hour by hand, and change the start-up cost. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. Number checks accept answers within 1%. Your answers stay in this browser until you finish.

What runs where. The RUN steps, and the slider, show results that milp_lab.py computed in advance, or a small calculation that this page does in JavaScript; no Python is installed or run on your device. To run the Python yourself, open the slides and press D on a slide marked “D · Python” (it runs in your browser), or run milp_lab.py in desktop Python.
