What is one more reactor hour worth?

About 40 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install; a calculator helps.

How this lab works

A weekly production plan for four products that share reactors, a distillation column and a feedstock supply. You read the optimal basis and its prices, check the certificate, then ask the question a plant manager asks: what is one more reactor hour worth, for how many hours, and is it worth renting more? Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. Numbers are accepted within about 1%; after two tries the expected value is shown.
