A practice review, and the review form

About 40 minutes, in class or on your own. Works on a laptop or a phone.

How this lab works

Part A is a practice review of a short report, Report D, a biodiesel feedstock blend (synthetic data). You mark the twelve checklist items, recompute three of the report's numbers, and write a short review. Part B is the review form, which you use on your card, on one of your own lab results, or on a classmate's card. Part C asks which item your card fails first. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. Numbers are accepted within about 1%; after two tries the expected value is shown.

What runs where. This lab runs no Python: you check a short report by reading it and recomputing three numbers with a calculator. review_checks.py has the same checks in code, for your own models.

Studying on your own? Watch the narrated slides for Session 15 first. The checks on this page come from review_checks.py, which you can reuse in your own work.
