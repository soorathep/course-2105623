Debug a colleague's flowsheet model

About 40 minutes. Works in class or on your own, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install.

How this lab works

A colleague wrote a linear model of a recycle process. HiGHS returns optimal, yet the model contains three planted errors: a units error, a missing equation and a sign error. You will find them with checks that do not need the right answer (dimensional consistency and plant-wide conservation), repair the model, and then verify the repaired optimum with a primal-dual certificate. Write each prediction before you look; the next step stays locked until you do. Number checks accept answers within 1%. Your answers stay in this browser until you finish.

Studying on your own because class was cancelled? Watch the narrated slides for Session 1 first, then come back here.

The specification your colleague was given

Isomerization A → B at steady state. Per-pass conversion of A is 60%. The separator recovers all B as product; unreacted A and the inert I are recycled, and 10% of the recycle loop is purged so that I cannot accumulate. Two fresh feeds are available. Maximize the hourly profit: product revenue minus feed cost minus purge disposal.

ItemValue

Restrictions (flows in kmol/h)

  1. R1 Feed A: FA = 0.90 F1 + 0.995 F2
  2. R2 Feed I: FI = 0.10 F1 + 0.005 F2
  3. R3 Mixer: AM = FA + RA and IM = FI + RI
  4. R4 Reactor: Aout = (1 − X) AM and B = X AM; I passes through
  5. R5 Purge: PA = φ Aout and PI = φ IM
  6. R6 Recycle: RA = (1 − φ) Aout and RI = (1 − φ) IM
  7. R7 Reactor inlet capacity: AM + IM ≤ 100
  8. R8 Market: B ≤ 40
  9. R9 All flows ≥ 0
