# Annual course archive

After each teaching year, review the final materials, remove student personal data and unpublished assessment answers, confirm reuse rights and license, and prepare a versioned archive with checksums. Upload to Zenodo only after owner review of the archive and metadata. Verify the published DOI and downloads before adding an annual archive link to the course website. Keep the current-year materials and a clear archive index. No Zenodo record has been published by this migration.
